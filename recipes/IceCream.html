Ice Cream

Description

A recipe on how to make homemade ice cream

Ingredients

  • 2¼ cups of milk
  • 1 cup of milk
  • Sugar
  • 2 teaspoons of vanilla extract

Directions

  • Cook the milk,cream, and sugar until the sugar has dissolved

  • transfer the mixture to a measuring cup, then add the vanilla extract. Chill overnight

  • Pour the mixture into an ice cream maker and churn according to the manufacturer's directions.

  • Ready to serve
